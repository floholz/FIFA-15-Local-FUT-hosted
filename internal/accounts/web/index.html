OUFT

Open Ultimate Football Team

OUFT is a small, invite-only community server for playing online matches with friends.

Got an invite link? Open it to create your account. Already have one? Log in to download OUFT Online.

Then

  1. Unzip the whole folder (keep payload next to the launcher).
  2. Start OUFT-Online.exe (Windows) or ./ouft-online (Linux).
  3. Log in with this account and press Play.
  4. In the game: Ultimate Team → Online → Single Match.

You need your own copy of the game, the same build as your friends.
